# uvarena Broadcast: tokens and components

Source of truth: the BC-*.dc.html boards on the canvas page "Broadcast" (generated by the design tooling, which holds every value below; the boards are not in this repo). Phone first, 390 x 844 reference; the laptop layout is the same column, centred, max 430 px wide.

## Colour

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| ground | #0E1013 | page background, dark text on coloured plates |
| plate | #1A1E24 | buttons, option plates, cards, clock plate |
| plate-dim | #15181D | revealed but irrelevant options, bottom nav, quiet panels, segmented track |
| line | #2A2F37 | hairlines, empty bar track, input border, 50:50 empty frame |
| edge | #08090B | 3 px bottom edge under plates |
| text | #F2F4F7 | body text; "correct" fill (with ground text) |
| muted | #8C939E | secondary text (5.5:1 on plate, 6:1 on ground) |
| faint | #5E6570 | only: the "?" of provisional ratings (24 px+ bold), spent power-ups, spent opponent kit icons |
| you | #3CE0C8 | your plate, bar, name tag, avatar; ground text on it |
| opponent | #FF4F5E | their plate, bar, tag; wrong-answer outline. Ground text only on it (white fails) |
| event | #FFD23F | primary button, multiplier plate, damage plate, armable frame, armed fill, picked power-up diamond, K.O. |

Rules: colour marks a player, never a result. Correct = text-colour fill with ground text and a check. Wrong = 2 px opponent-red inset outline, red x, red strikethrough. Whose pick = name tag. At rest, at most one yellow element per screen (the primary button, or the multiplier during a match). Gradients, glow and photos appear only inside power-up banners.

Uppercase only on name plates, name tags and callouts. Never on questions, options or buttons.

## Spacing and shape

Scale 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 40. Side margin 16. Option gap 8 to 10. Block gap 16 in a match, 12 to 14 on forms. Corners: plates and buttons 2 px, options 3 px; avatars are the only round shapes. Touch targets 44 px minimum; options 52 to 60 px; power-ups 48 px.

**The 12 degree cut.** Only the inner ends of the two HUD name plates (and the versus seam). `clip-path: polygon(0 0, 100% 0, calc(100% - 14px) 100%, 0 100%)` for you, mirrored `polygon(0 0, 100% 0, 100% 100%, 14px 100%)` for them: 14 px over 64 px = 12.3 degrees. Inner padding 20 px on the cut side, 12 px on the outer side.

## HUD strip (fixed through arming, banners, question, reveal)

- 44 px top row: mode and round ("Ranked · Round 6", 14 muted), sound and surrender (hold 1 s) as 44 px icon buttons.
- Grid `1fr 64px 1fr`, column gap 4, height 64: your plate, clock plate, their plate. Name top, HP bottom, aligned to the outer edge for the name and to the clock for the HP.
- Answered: 14 px check before their name (after yours). Disconnected: plate turns plate-colour with muted text and a wifi-off icon, their bar at 35% opacity.
- Clock plate: plate colour, text digits. Under 5 s: opponent red with ground digits. After you answered: muted digits, frozen.
- Row 2 (28 px): 8 px square-ended bars, filling outward from the clock (yours right-anchored, theirs left-anchored), 2 px ground ticks at every 1,000 HP (16.67%). Multiplier plate under the clock only when above x1: 22 px high, yellow, Condensed 18 700, "x2.5". Under their bar: their three power-ups as 14 px muted icons, spent ones faint.

## Event lane

The flexible space between HUD and question card. It takes the spare height and is where events land, so nothing floats over text:
- at rest: "ROUND 6" in Condensed italic 32 muted;
- opponent answered: "10 SECONDS" outline callout + "kvdberg answered" (5 SECONDS under Gotta Go Fast);
- hits: the damage plate; K.O.;
- quick power-up banners (50:50, Gotta Go Fast) replace the lane as a full-bleed strip, min 112 px;
- disconnect: "kvdberg disconnected", "0:38" Condensed 56, one muted line.

## Question card and options

Card: plate, radius 3, padding 20/16/18, 4 px drain bar across the top edge (line track, text-colour fill, opponent red under 5 s), drains left to right over the question time. "Select all that apply" is the only label (15 600) and only on multiple select. Cloze gap: an 88 x 4 px text-colour rule. The card, options and power-ups form one block anchored to the bottom.

Options: full width, plate fill, `box-shadow: 0 3px 0 #08090B`, 17/24 500, left aligned, padding 8/16.
- Press: translateY(3px) and drop the shadow, 60 ms.
- Correct: text fill, ground text, check, `0 3px 0 #8C939E`.
- Wrong: plate, inset 2 px red, red x, struck text.
- Others after reveal: plate-dim, muted text, 46 px left padding so text aligns with the icons above.
- 50:50 removed: transparent with a 1 px line inset frame, empty, disabled.
- Multiple select: 22 px square checkbox (2 px muted outline; checked = text fill with ground check) and a 2 px text inset outline on the option; `aria-pressed`. Then the primary "Lock in".
- True/false: two 200 px plates side by side, Condensed 36 700.
- Name tags on revealed options: YOU (you colour) or the opponent's name (red), Condensed 15 700.

## Buttons

- Primary: yellow plate, ground text, Condensed 24 700, 52 to 60 px high, wrapped in 3 px padding with `outline: 1px solid #FFD23F; outline-offset: 3px`. Press: fill and frame drop 3 px together, 60 ms. Disabled: line fill, muted text, no frame. One per screen.
- Secondary: plate with 3 px edge, Barlow 16 600, 48 to 52 px. Press: translateY(3px), shadow 0.
- Text button: transparent, text colour, 16 600, 48 px.
- Switch: 48 x 28, radius 2. On = text track, ground knob right. Off = line track, muted knob left. `role="switch"` on a button.
- Segmented: plate-dim track, 3 px inset, active segment line fill with text colour.
- Chip (lecture filter): 40 px, plate with 1 px line inset, chevron.

## Power-ups

Bottom row, 3 columns, gap 12, 48 px.
- Not usable now: plate, muted. Usable now (armable in arming, quick during the question): plate, text colour, 1 px yellow frame offset 3 px. Armed or firing: solid yellow, ground text. Spent: transparent, 1 px line inset, faint, struck.
- Picker (Hades rows): 88 px rows; 34 px diamond (rotated square) with the glyph; picked = yellow diamond, plate row with edge, checked box; unpicked = 2 px faint outline diamond, 1 px line frame row, empty box. Effect line 14 muted with the numbers in text colour 600. Picks save on tap; "3 of 4" in the bar.

## Banners (1.5 s, the one loud place)

- Blind (Bibi, Shield): fill everything between the HUD and the power-up row; HUD and power-up row stay.
- Bibi: radial gold glow on #0B0C0F, pale bolt down the middle, the face 300 px (asset `bibi.png`), then "BENJAMIN NETANYAHU, STRIKE HIM DOWN!" Condensed italic 60/54 white with gold glow (`0 0 18px rgba(255,210,63,.9), 0 0 42px rgba(255,170,30,.6), 0 3px 0 #000`), overlapping the face by 64 px. Who armed it as a name tag on top.
- Shield: plate-dim panel, 88 px outline shield, "Shield up." Condensed 64 upright, "Damage halved this round." 17 muted. No glow, no joke.
- Quick (50:50, Gotta Go Fast): a full-bleed strip in the event lane; the question stays readable.
- 50:50: navy to violet stage (`#050A2C` to `#10195A`, violet glow from below, 2 px #7F8CFF top and bottom lines), logo left 104 px, host right 128 px bottom-aligned, "FIFTY FIFTY." Condensed 44 white, violet glow.
- Gotta Go Fast: blue speed blur (`#06167A`, `#0D3BE0`, `#2F7BFF`), 2 px horizontal scan lines every 15 px, white streaks fading in from the left, triple chevron, "GOTTA GO FAST." Condensed italic 54 with a 4 px hard navy shadow.

## Damage plate

Yellow, drops from under the clock into the event lane. Normal: "−1,800" Condensed 32 + "You 0 · kvdberg 900 · x2" 14 600. Bibi round: twice the size, "−3,600" at 64 with a dark "BIBI x2" tag on top and "x2 · Bibi x2" in the line. Lost HP on your bar shows as an opponent-red chunk on the outer side of the remaining fill; on their bar the chunk is text-white (red would vanish into their red bar).

## Per screen

- **Duel**: event lane absorbs spare height; card, options and power-ups bottom-anchored; no void wider than the lane's own content.
- **Arming (3 s)**: clock plate counts 3; lane shows "ROUND 6" 80 + yellow "x2" plate, a 200 px drain line, "Arm a blind power-up"; Bibi and Shield framed, quick power-ups plain.
- **True/false**: statement 28/36 in the card, buttons 200 px. 50:50 stays disabled.
- **Disconnected**: the match keeps running; their plate goes grey with the wifi-off mark; lane holds the countdown.
- **K.O.**: the reveal stays under it at full contrast; the yellow K.O. is the only event word.
- **End (loss, bot)**: VICTORY / DEFEAT, both final HP in player colours under name tags, rating card with one big change number and a rank bar (1,000 to 2,200 scale, moved segment, 3 px marker). Bot end adds per-answer rating bars (up in text colour, down in faint) and the unlocked next bot; primary plays it.
- **Landing**: wordmark, one line in Condensed 56, a live HUD strip trading a hit (damage plate plus the revealed correct option with a name tag), Sign up primary, Log in secondary, Credits link. No drawing of the app.
- **Sign up / Log in / Onboarding**: labelled inputs (48 px, 1 px line inset, 2 px text inset on focus), hints 14 muted under the field; Google button secondary.
- **Home**: unverified banner is a 1 px line frame row with Resend; everything else as B2-Home.
- **Bot ladder**: Echo on top as a plate; beaten bots with a check; next bot in a 2 px text-outlined plate with a white "Play" chip; locked ones muted with a lock and not focusable.
- **Queue**: the HUD strip pre-forms with your plate and an empty "SEARCHING ?" plate; the clock counts up; rating window bar; Cancel secondary. At 20 s: "Nobody else is searching." with Send a friend a link (primary), Play a bot instead, Keep waiting.
- **Opponent found**: outline callout, their name plate 56 italic, rating, your record against them, their loadout; 2 s drain to the intro.
- **Duel a friend**: segmented Create/Join; code as 6 plates (Condensed 48), link with copy; Share primary; lectures, power-ups, rated settings; expiry line. Join: one 80 px input, letter-spacing 0.42em, hint about 0, O, 1, I.
- **Lobby**: both players as plates with Ready state and loadouts, "VS" muted between, settings list including "Game 2 of 5 with kvdberg today", Ready primary.
- **Missed list / detail / retry**: lecture chip + To review/Learned segment, count + Retry these; cards clamp to 3 lines. Detail: right answer filled, your pick outlined red with YOU, Why and Why not, Read in the notes (external), Mark as learned primary. Retry: progress line, untimed, no drain bar, "Just show me".
- **Leaderboard**: ranked players first; "Still provisional" section with "?" ratings; your row outlined in your colour.
- **Profiles**: ranked and bot ratings as two plates; played/won/lost/drawn as four numbers; accuracy per lecture as 6 px bars (below 60% in muted). Public profile adds head-to-head (7 · 4 in player colours with a split bar) and Duel kvdberg as primary.
- **History / round detail**: grouped by day; rounds show multiplier, power-up used, damage coloured by whose HP it hit, both answers with times.
- **Settings / Credits**: avatar with Add a picture, names, Save primary, sound switch, email verification, Log out, Credits link; credits list each image with its thumbnail, author, licence and source.
